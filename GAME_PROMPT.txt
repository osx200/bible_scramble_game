BIBLE GAMES - FULL BUILD PROMPT
================================

Use this prompt to rebuild, extend or brief someone on the Bible Games app exactly as it
exists today. It describes the purpose, every game mode, the rules, the data, the design
system, the backend and the deployment.


1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
-----------------------
Build "Bible Games": a web app that keeps church fellowship meetings lively and engaging
while keeping hearts and minds focused on God. It is played by individuals on their phones
and by groups around a laptop, TV or projector, often with a moderator running the session.

Anchor verse (shown on the welcome page, King James Version, public domain):
  "And let us consider one another to provoke unto love and to good works: Not forsaking
  the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some is; but exhorting one
  another: and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching." Hebrews 10:24-25 (KJV)

Contact for suggestions: bible-games@proton.me

Disclaimer (shown on the welcome page and the About view):
  Bible Games is a free, independent resource for fellowship and personal study, and is
  not affiliated with any church or denomination. It is meant to support Bible reading,
  not replace it. Names you enter may appear on the public monthly leaderboard, so please
  avoid full names or personal details, especially for children. Scripture quoted from the
  King James Version (public domain).


2. TECH STACK AND CONSTRAINTS
-----------------------------
- React 18 + Vite 5, plain JavaScript (JSX). No router: navigation is state-driven.
- CSS Modules for every component, all colours/spacing/radii as CSS custom properties in
  one tokens file. Never hardcode colours.
- No UI library. No icon library. Emoji are NOT used as interface icons (only for
  celebrations and results).
- Backend: a tiny Node 24 HTTP server using only built-ins (node:http + node:sqlite).
  Zero npm dependencies on the server.
- Do not use crypto.randomUUID (the app may run over plain HTTP). Use:
    uid = Date.now().toString(36) + Math.random().toString(36).slice(2)
- Mobile-first: test at 390px wide. Use 100dvh, never 100vh. Inputs at least 16px font so
  iOS does not zoom. Hover effects only inside @media (hover: hover).
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Full keyboard focus styles.
- Light and dark theme, toggled by a round button (top-right), saved in localStorage.


3. FIRST RUN: WELCOME PAGE AND PLAYER PROFILE
---------------------------------------------
- On first visit the app shows a Welcome page instead of any game.
- It contains: the "Welcome" label, the title "Bible Games", a short purpose paragraph,
  the Hebrews 10:24-25 verse, a "Who's playing?" form, the suggestions email and the
  disclaimer.
- Form: choose Individual or Group (segmented toggle), then a name (2 to 24 characters,
  whitespace collapsed, control characters stripped). Button: "Let's Play".
- The profile { name, kind: 'individual' | 'group' } is saved in localStorage ("player").
- The name personalises the whole app. There is no fixed product brand:
    - Individuals get a possessive: "Kwame's Bible Games", "James' Bible Games".
    - Groups use the plain name: "Grace Fellowship Bible Games".
    - Browser tab title follows the same rule ("Bible Games" before a name is set).
- The home menu shows the name as a small caps line above a huge "Bible Games" wordmark,
  a "Welcome back! Choose a game to get started." line, and a "Playing as a group /
  an individual - Change" chip that reopens a compact name form (with Cancel).
- The name also appears on the solo game board, Game Over ("Well done, NAME!"), Recall
  results, the Quiz summary, and pre-fills Team 1 when the profile is a group.
- An "About and suggestions" link on the menu reopens the purpose, verse, email and
  disclaimer.


4. HOME MENU
------------
A table-of-contents style list (not a grid of tiles). Each row: big title, one-line
description, arrow. Rows:
  1. Word Unscramble - "Unscramble books, prophets, disciples and Bible couples. Solo or
     teams, three difficulty levels."
  2. Book Recall - "Name as many books of the Bible as you can before the timer runs out."
  3. Moderator View - "Host a live group session - reveal answers on demand with reactions."
  4. Quiz & Scramble Builder - "Make your own quiz or word scramble, typed in or uploaded
     from Excel, and host it for your group."
Below the list, a solid accent-coloured bar: "Monthly Leaderboard - Top 5 players and
groups. Resets on the 1st." Then the "About and suggestions" link.


5. GAME MODE 1: WORD UNSCRAMBLE (the main game)
-----------------------------------------------
Flow: choose a word set -> setup -> play -> results.

5a. Word sets (four)
  - Bible Books: the names of the books of the Bible (numbered books keep their number,
    e.g. "1 Samuel"; numbers are required when answering).
  - Prophets & Disciples: Old and New Testament prophets (about 49) plus the Twelve,
    Matthias and Paul: Peter, Andrew, James, John, Thomas, Matthew, Paul (easy);
    Bartholomew, Thaddaeus, Matthias, Judas Iscariot (medium); Simon the Zealot,
    James son of Alphaeus (hard). Philip appears once even though he is on both lists.
  - Biblical Couples: one spouse is shown, players unscramble the other, e.g.
    "MOSES & ________" with the missing name as scrambled tiles. Which spouse is shown is
    random each round ("________ & ZIPPORAH" also happens). The underscores match the
    answer's length. Hint line: "Unscramble the name of Moses' spouse". After answering,
    feedback and results show the whole pair ("Moses & Zipporah"). 36 pairs:
      Easy: Adam & Eve, Abraham & Sarah, Isaac & Rebekah, Jacob & Rachel, Boaz & Ruth,
        Moses & Zipporah, Elkanah & Hannah, David & Bathsheba, Ahab & Jezebel,
        Joseph & Mary, Zechariah & Elizabeth, Aquila & Priscilla
      Medium: Jacob & Leah, David & Michal, David & Abigail, Hosea & Gomer,
        Ananias & Sapphira, Xerxes & Esther, Joseph & Asenath, Amram & Jochebed,
        Elimelech & Naomi, Nabal & Abigail, Salmon & Rahab, Herod & Herodias
      Hard: Aaron & Elisheba, Heber & Jael, Lappidoth & Deborah, Abraham & Keturah,
        Mahlon & Ruth, Jehoiada & Jehosheba, Felix & Drusilla, Xerxes & Vashti,
        Uriah & Bathsheba, Lamech & Adah, Esau & Judith, Er & Tamar
    Couples has no Old/New Testament filter (too few NT couples).
  - Grand Mix: Books + Prophets & Disciples + Couples in one pool (duplicates such as
    Amos the book and Amos the prophet appear once; "Ruth" the book and "Boaz & Ruth"
    both stay). Couple rounds show their prompt inside the mix.
  Every entry has: name, testament (OT/NT), difficulty (1 easy, 2 medium, 3 hard),
  wordCount. Keep each (category x difficulty) pool at 10+ entries so teams do not
  repeat names.

5b. Setup
  - Game mode: Solo or Teams.
  - Difficulty: Easy / Medium / Hard. Difficulty is cumulative when drawing words
    (Hard includes easy and medium words).
  - Category: All / Old Testament / New Testament (hidden for Couples).
  - Seconds per round: slider 5 to 120 with -/+ buttons. Defaults: Easy 45s, Medium 30s,
    Hard 20s.
  - Button: "Start Game" (solo) or "Set Up Teams" (teams).

5c. Scrambling
  - Each word is scrambled separately with Fisher-Yates, numeric prefixes kept in place,
    retried up to 10 times so the scramble never equals the original.
  - Letters appear as large tiles that scale with the screen (big enough for a
    projector), dropping in with a short stagger.

5d. Rules and scoring (flat)
  - 10 rounds per game (per team in team mode).
  - Correct answer: +20 points. Wrong answer, timeout or skip: -2 points. The total can
    go negative. Difficulty and streaks never change points.
  - Answers are compared case-insensitively with whitespace collapsed.
  - A streak counter and best streak are tracked.
  - Hints are free: Easy shows First Letter, Testament, Word Count; Medium shows First
    Letter and Word Count; Hard has no hints.
  - Controls: Pause/Resume, Skip (counts as wrong), Stop Game (or End Tournament).
  - After each answer a feedback overlay shows correct/incorrect, points and
    "The answer was: X" for 1.5 seconds before the next round.

5e. Voice answers
  - A microphone button uses the Web Speech API (needs HTTPS or localhost).
  - The engine's ranked alternatives are resolved to a valid name: the whole phrase is
    tried first, then filler like "the book of", "the prophet", "the apostle" is
    stripped, spoken numbers become digits ("first Samuel" -> "1 Samuel"), and a
    Levenshtein fuzzy match is used as a last resort (never crossing a number prefix).
    Fuzzy matches ask for confirmation instead of auto-submitting.

5f. Teams mode
  - Register 2 to 6 teams, each with a name and colour; names must be unique.
  - Teams alternate one round at a time, each playing 10 rounds. A transition screen
    shows whose turn is next and the running scoreboard.
  - Team Results ranks teams with medals, celebrates the winner with a trophy reaction
    and confetti (a tie gets a handshake instead), and offers "Play Again (Same Teams)".

5g. Solo results (Game Over)
  - Rating by % correct: 100% Perfect score (trophy, winner reaction), 80%+ Excellent,
    60%+ Well done, 40%+ Keep studying, otherwise More practice needed. Confetti at 80%+.
  - Stats: Score, Correct x/10, Best Streak. Round-by-round list with time used and
    points.
  - Leaderboard strip (see section 9): saving status, "You're #2 on this month's Top
    Players!" banner when placed, and an inline "View leaderboard" toggle.


6. GAME MODE 2: BOOK RECALL
---------------------------
- Name as many of the 66 books of the Bible as possible before time runs out.
- Setup: All 66 / Old Testament (39) / New Testament (27); time limit 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes.
- Free typing with an alias matcher (Roman numerals, "1st John", abbreviations).
- Hands-free voice: one tap on the mic keeps it listening for the whole round (it
  restarts itself when the browser pauses after silence) and closes when time runs out.
  Players can say several books in one breath ("Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus"; "first John,
  second John, third John"). Feedback lists what was added and notes duplicates or books
  outside the chosen category; a live line shows what was heard.
  Unrecognised input shakes with "Not a Bible book". Duplicates are ignored.
- Results: count and percentage, which books were named and which were missed, split
  into Old and New Testament.
- Uses the true 66-book canon list (kept separate from the scramble word list).
- Recall does not post to the leaderboard and does not auto-fire reactions.


7. GAME MODE 3: MODERATOR VIEW
------------------------------
- For a host running a group session on a big screen.
- Setup: category (All / OT / NT), difficulty, number of words (5, 10, 15, 20), time per
  word (15s, 30s, 45s, 1 min, 90s).
- Play screen: the scrambled word in large type, a hidden "Correct answer" tile revealed
  by "SHOW ANSWER", a countdown with -/+ 15s adjust, START and STOP, Prev/Next and a dot
  per word for jumping, and an inline reactions panel.
- If a word has a clue (custom words from the builder), a "Show clue" button reveals it.


8. GAME MODE 4: QUIZ & SCRAMBLE BUILDER
---------------------------------------
- Two tabs: Quiz and Scramble.
- Quiz tab: add questions by hand (Question, Answer, optional Bible Reference), load 10
  sample questions, or bulk upload. Start Quiz runs a host-led player: one question at a
  time, a reveal button for the answer and reference, mark correct/wrong, and a summary
  (trophy at 80%+).
- Scramble tab: add words by hand (Word, optional Clue, Difficulty Auto/Easy/Medium/Hard),
  load 10 sample words with clues, or bulk upload. Before starting, choose which
  difficulty to play (All/Easy/Medium/Hard, each showing its count), time per word and
  shuffle. Start Scramble runs the Moderator play screen with the custom words.
- Bulk upload:
    - "Download Excel template" link to a prebuilt .xlsx with three sheets:
      Instructions (how to fill it in, with examples), Scramble (Word | Clue | Difficulty,
      with an Easy/Medium/Hard dropdown) and Quiz (Question | Answer | Bible Reference).
      Bold frozen header rows.
    - Upload .xlsx or .csv by file picker or drag and drop. Headers match
      case-insensitively and in any order; one workbook works from both tabs.
    - Choose "Add to list" or "Replace" when the list already has items.
    - Result summary: "18 words added - 2 rows skipped" with the real Excel row number and
      reason for each skipped row (empty word, duplicate, bad difficulty, too long, etc.).
    - Up to 300 rows per upload. Words must be 2 to 30 characters with at least two
      letters. Blank difficulty is guessed from length (<=5 letters easy, <=8 medium,
      else hard).
    - The .xlsx is read in the browser with no library (hand-walked zip +
      DecompressionStream('deflate-raw') + DOMParser).
- SESSION ONLY: builder content lives in memory for that moderator session and is
  discarded when the builder is closed. Never saved to the browser or the server. Leaving
  with content asks for confirmation. Custom games never reach the leaderboard.


9. MONTHLY LEADERBOARD
----------------------
- Two shared boards: Top Players (individuals) and Top Groups. Top 5 each.
- Only Word Unscramble posts scores (all four word sets).
  - Solo games post to the profile's board (a group playing solo lands on Top Groups).
  - Team games post every team to Top Groups under the team name.
- One row per name (best score, case-insensitive). Ties go to the harder difficulty,
  then to whoever scored first.
- Monthly reset without a scheduled job: every score is stamped with its month and the
  board only shows the current month (timezone configurable, default UTC). Old months are
  kept for a possible "last month's champion" view.
- Each row shows medal or rank, name (with a "You" tag for the current player), score and
  "x/10 correct - Difficulty - Word set". Header: month name and "resets 1 Nov".
- States: loading skeleton, empty ("No scores yet this month. Be the first!"), offline
  ("Leaderboard unavailable right now. Please try again in a little while."). A dead
  server never breaks a game.

Leaderboard API (server/index.js):
  GET  /api/health
  GET  /api/leaderboard?board=individual|group[&month=YYYY-MM]
  POST /api/scores  { id, board, name, score, correct, total, difficulty, wordSet }
  DELETE /api/scores/:id   header X-Admin-Token (disabled unless ADMIN_TOKEN is set)
  - The server recomputes every score from correct/total (20 / -2) and rejects any that
    do not match. Validates board, difficulty, wordSet (books, prophets, couples,
    grandmix), totals 1 to 10, names 2 to 24 characters, and a profanity blocklist
    (whole-word and squashed-fragment matching, leetspeak undone).
  - Client-generated ids make resubmission idempotent. Scores of 0 or less are not stored.
  - Returns { month, rank } so the end screen can announce a placing.
  - Rate limit: 30 posts per 10 minutes per IP (first X-Forwarded-For hop), 2 KB body
    limit, Cache-Control: no-store.
  - SQLite file on a Docker named volume so scores survive rebuilds.


10. REACTIONS AND CELEBRATIONS
------------------------------
- Reactions: Confetti, Winner (trophy, reserved for real wins), High Five, Applause,
  On Fire, Amazing, Love It, Party. Each shows a large animated overlay; some add confetti.
- A floating collapsible picker is available on every screen (the moderator screen has its
  own inline panel instead).
- Automatic: a random reaction on each correct answer in Word Unscramble and Quiz (never
  the same twice in a row, never the trophy). The trophy fires only for a perfect solo
  game, a clear team winner or a quiz at 80%+.
- The overlay never blocks clicks.


11. VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM: "LAPIS & INK"
--------------------------------------
Goal: look crafted and church-appropriate, never AI-generated.

Colours (one accent only):
  Light: background #EEF0F3, card #F9FAFB, surface #E6EAF0, text #12162A,
         muted text #4C5468, placeholder #5F667B, border #D3D8E1,
         primary/accent lapis #23409A (lighter #2F55C4), success #2A7449,
         danger #B0302B.
  Dark ("night ink"): background #0C0F1A, card #151A2B, surface #1C2236,
         text #E9ECF5, muted #A3AAC0, primary lapis #8EA6FF, success #6CC48E,
         danger #F07A6E.
  All text passes WCAG AA (4.5:1), placeholders included.

Fonts (sans-serif only, self-hosted, no Google Fonts request):
  - Cabinet Grotesk 700/800 for headings and big numbers.
  - Satoshi 400/500/700 for text.
  (Fontshare, ITF Free Font License.)

House rules:
  - Flat fills. No gradient buttons, no coloured glows. Emphasis = solid fill or a 3px rule.
  - Radii: panels 16px, buttons/inputs/tiles 10px, chips 6px, round icon buttons full.
  - Left-aligned headings. Small uppercase label above a title only on the home masthead
    and the welcome page.
  - No emoji as UI icons. No em-dashes in visible text.
  - Tabular numbers for timers and scores.

Full-screen layout ("Stage"):
  - Setup, menu and result screens are a full-viewport split: a full-height engraving on
    the left (about 40%), content on the right. Under 900px the art becomes a band across
    the top and the content stacks below.
  - In-game screens (game board, moderator play, quiz player, recall play) use the full
    width and full height with no artwork, so a projector shows only the game.
  - Every screen fits a 900px-tall laptop screen without scrolling (the builder excepted).

12. DEPLOYMENT
--------------
- Docker Compose with two services:
    bible-scramble: multi-stage build (Node 24 builds the Vite app, nginx 1.27 serves it),
      bound to 127.0.0.1:8080. nginx proxies /api/ to the api service via Docker DNS with
      a variable upstream so the site still boots if the API is down.
    api: the Node leaderboard server, no published ports, SQLite on the
      "leaderboard-data" named volume, env ADMIN_TOKEN (optional) and BOARD_TZ.
- Production: EC2 host nginx terminates HTTPS (DuckDNS subdomain + Let's Encrypt) and
  reverse-proxies to the container. Permissions-Policy must keep microphone=(self) or
  voice input breaks. index.html is served no-cache.
- Home network: a LAN override serves HTTPS with an mkcert certificate so phones get
  microphone access; the local CA is downloadable over plain HTTP.
- Dev: "npm run dev" (Vite, proxies /api) and "npm run api" (leaderboard server).
